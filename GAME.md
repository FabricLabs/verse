## Overview
VERSE is a robust rogue-like RPG in which the player controls an immortal soul and accumulates various resources and upgrades as they navigate an endless series of procedurally-generated and even player-owned worlds.

## Design
1. The player begins by creating a name, which is used to identify their character in the world
2. The game fades to a narrative experience, introducing the first world: The Garden
3. "The Garden" is a tutorial map in which enemy "mobs" spawn at the 5 minute mark if the user has not yet completed the "Humble Beginnings" quest
4. If the user fails to kill all mobs before the 10 minute mark, "Game Over" and allow the player to try again (using the same character name)
5. The player's character takes control of a mechanical apparatus known as "The Farmer" with its own set of base statistics and equipment (inherits from same root Entity class)
6. The "control" mechanic is fundamental to the game; as a soul, the player may inhabit any entity with sufficient level or exit the entity (including "The Farmer" in this case).  If the player's currently controlled entity is destroyed (for example, if it reaches HP 0), they are ejected into the world as their spirit with a 300 second "Divine Intervention" buff granting invulnerability, with a cooldown of 24 hours.
7. Upon completing the first quest ("Humble Beginnings") the game is paused and the user is presented with a "navigation screen" enabling movement to the next world, their "Home World" — this is attached as an "edge" to the user's "Farm" map, which is permanently saved upon quest completion.  The navigation screen is generally available during play, and lists all visible "edges" to the current world, regardless of their direction or distance from the player's character.
8. Worlds which are "edges" to the current should be loaded into the client and visible when approaching the boundary of the current world (or at all times for worlds smaller than 64x64x64).  When anything crosses the boundary of a world and would enter another world, insert that actor to the destination world at the correct first position.
9. Certain tiles within the world can be "portals" to other worlds, exactly like an edge.
10. Direct edges are named a "up" "down" "east" "west" "north" and "south" but also the diagonal worlds should be loaded (at all corners, for a total of 27 worlds for a cube of 3x3x3 worlds with the player's current position at the center).
11. When the player's character would move into a portal tile, pause the game and prompt the user to confirm.
12. When reaching the "Home" location for the first time, introduce the user to "building" — the reward from the first quest should include a stone block, which is then placed as the first step towards "Creating a Legacy" (the next quest, which requires completion of a statue).
13. All edges of the player's home world are pre-generated by default, and the player is encouraged to explore.  As the player navigates these worlds, "random encounters" take place and begin a new "battle stage" with optional dialogue overlaid before combat begins.
14. These battles are on a timer that begins when the player selects "Begin" on an "enemy overview" after the optional dialogue; the maximum time is 10 minutes.

### Character
The player controls an immortal soul which retains a permanent set of attributes.

#### Attributes
- Strength — hit points and melee damage
- Dexterity — armor class / damage reduction, turn speed, and stamina
- Intelligence — mana pool and skill/spell power (fireball damage, ice chill, water volume)
- Wisdom — detection range and effective luck
- Constitution — carry weight / toughness checks
- Luck — fortune rolls (boosted by wisdom)

#### Leveling (DOTA-style)
Each level grants flat growth to HP, stamina, and mana, plus one attribute point.
Spending points on STR / DEX / INT further raises those pools (and DEX also raises armor and turn rate).
Intelligence multiplies skill potency (INT 10 = 1.0x, INT 20 = 2.0x).

### Homeworld
The player is granted a personal world, with permanent ownership assigned.

### Exploration
The player may embark on an "adventure" which teleports the soul to a world outside of their homeworld.

#### Encounters
While exploring, players may encounter events triggered by their presence.  Encounters begin battle mode, which may include combat.  Players may receive experience points during or after combat, which can be used to improve their character.
